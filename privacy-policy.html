SpinPop is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used SpinPop, contact us and we will help.

What SpinPop does not collect

This list is written from the code, not from intent:

  • No account. There is no sign-up, no email, no password, no profile.
  • No name, address, phone number, date of birth or photograph.
  • No location — not GPS, not coarse, not IP-derived by us.
  • No contacts, camera, microphone or photo library. The app requests none of these permissions.
  • No sale of personal data.

We do not operate a server that holds your data. Your save file lives on your device and we cannot read it.

Ads

SpinPop is free and shows ads to stay that way.

  • Banner ads appear only on the gameplay screen, in a strip reserved at its top or bottom; they never cover the game. There are no unskippable full-screen ads.
  • Some rewards are offered in exchange for watching a short video. These are always optional: declining costs you nothing and the whole game remains playable.
  • With your permission ads are personalised using your advertising identifier; without it you see non-personalised ads.
  • Ad content is capped at a PG rating with our advertising partner.

Our advertising partner is Appodeal (Appodeal Inc.), which mediates several ad networks. Its privacy policy: https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy. In regions where the law requires it, Appodeal's own consent dialog may also ask for your choices; it is a certified consent manager and honours what you answer.

Leaderboards

On iOS, SpinPop's leaderboards (Endless Run and Daily Challenge) use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted under the name Game Center shows, governed by Apple's privacy policy. To draw the leaderboard screen, the game reads the ranks, scores and display names Game Center returns; it does not store them or send them anywhere else. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your mind at any time: Settings → Privacy options re-opens the consent question.
  • Turn off personalised ads at the system level: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling SpinPop removes the save file and every local record. There is no server-side copy to delete. For anything held by Apple, Google or Appodeal, use their channels (linked above).
  • Depending on where you live (GDPR in the EU/EEA and UK, KVKK in Türkiye, CCPA in California) you may also have rights of access, correction, portability, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority. Contact us at support.spinpop@gmail.com and we will honour them.

Children

See "Who SpinPop is for". If a store or a regulator determines that our audience includes children under 13, we will switch every install to non-personalised ads and disable analytics; the game is built so that this is a configuration change, not a rebuild.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we raise the in-app consent version, which asks you the question again rather than assuming your old answer still applies. The date at the top is the date of the current version.

Support

Questions, problems, or anything that looks broken — write to support.spinpop@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

To help us find it faster, tell us your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

I paid but did not get my purchase

Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so this works on a new device or after reinstalling.

Booster packs and the Starter Pack are consumable: they can be bought again, and what they gave you is not restored on a new device or after reinstalling. Remove Banners is permanent and always comes back.

If a charge went through and the item still has not arrived, email us with the receipt Apple sent you and we will sort it out.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored on your device, not on a server. Deleting the app deletes it, and it does not transfer to a new phone.

Remove Banners is the exception: it lives with your Apple ID and comes back with Restore Purchases.

An ad will not load, or the reward did not arrive

Rewarded videos need a connection, and there is not always one available to show. If a video fails or you close it early, no reward is given and nothing is taken from you — the offer simply comes back later.

If a video played all the way through and the reward still did not arrive, email us and tell us which reward it was.

I bought Remove Banners but still see ads

Remove Banners removes the banner strip. Rewarded videos stay, because they are the ones you choose to watch in exchange for something.

If banners are still showing after the purchase, tap Restore Purchases first. If they persist after that, email us.

How do I change my ads or privacy choice

Settings, then Privacy options. You can change the personalised-ads answer there at any time, and the game stays fully playable either way.

I want my data deleted

There is no account and no server-side profile, so there is nothing stored about you to delete on our side. Deleting the app removes everything the game kept on your device.

If you want the details of what is collected and by whom, that is in the privacy policy.

The game will not start, or crashes

Restart the device and make sure iOS and the app are up to date. If it still happens, email us with your device model and iOS version — that is usually enough for us to reproduce it.
